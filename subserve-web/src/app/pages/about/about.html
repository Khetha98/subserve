Empowering Township Commerce & Communities

About Subserve Digital

Subserve Digital (Pty) Ltd is a technology-first software consultancy and digital services provider. We deliver enterprise-grade software engineering, open-source ERP systems, and accessible digital admin tools directly to township SMMEs and local residents.

Our Mission

To bridge the digital divide across South African townships by deploying affordable, high-reliability software solutions—from anti-theft B&B booking engines and custom POS receipt systems to guided public helpdesk services—empowering local businesses to operate securely and scale.

What We Do

01

SMME Software & POS

Custom Odoo and ERPNext POS implementations, thermal receipt formatting, and anti-theft cash audit trails for local traders, taverns, and B&Bs.

02

Public Digital Helpdesk

Guided assistance for residents accessing e-government platforms, including eHomeAffairs appointments, SARS eFiling, PSIRA verifications, and SASSA grant tracking.

03

IT Consulting & Document Hub

OS clean installs (Windows/Linux), hardware troubleshooting, professional ATS CV design, A4/A3 color printing, and heavy-duty 250-micron lamination.

Our Hub Footprint

Operating through single-operator digital hub containers and local office spaces across Soweto (Gauteng), Umlazi (KZN), and Khayelitsha (Western Cape), backed by nationwide remote software consulting.
